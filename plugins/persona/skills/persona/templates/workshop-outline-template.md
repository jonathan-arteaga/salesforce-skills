## Run of show

Choose only activities that help answer relevant questions not yet settled by the requester's input. No decision tracker or prior persona draft is needed. Individual/pair input followed by shared dot placement is one useful pattern, not a required sequence; adjust timing and remove unused activities.

### 1. Frame the decision ({{MINUTES}})

{{FACILITATOR PROMPT ABOUT THE AGENT, AUDIENCE, AND WHAT IS KNOWN VERSUS OPEN}}

- Optional opening contrast: show one caller line and two or three agent replies to the same moment. Ask "What differences do you see? Which response fits {{BRAND}} and which would you change?" Hold facts and permitted actions constant. Do not make promises about access, timing, or resolution that the agent cannot support.

### 2. Explore identity individually or in pairs ({{MINUTES}})

{{PRINTABLE_OR_VIRTUAL_INPUT_AREA_FOR_IDENTITY, PERSONIFICATION, AND_REGISTER; USE_ONLY_RELEVANT_QUESTIONS}}

- For an open brand-identity question, give each person a space in the good and bad columns. Ask "What felt right for {{BRAND}} in a customer interaction? What did the person say or do?" and "What would feel wrong for {{BRAND}}? What words or behaviors should the agent avoid?" Collect concrete incidents or phrases rather than generic adjectives. Discuss the patterns and disagreement.
- Prompt: {{WHAT_KIND_OF_ASSISTANT_SHOULD_USERS_EXPERIENCE_AND_WHY; OPTIONAL_CHARACTER_SPECTRUM}}
- Optional: invite names or avatar sketches only if a distinct identity is genuinely open. A sketch is an elicitation device, not an approved visual design.
- Facilitator action: {{COMPARE_ANSWERS; IF_PAIR_PARTNERS_DISAGREE, KEEP_BOTH_VIEWS_AND_REASONS}}

### 3. Test difficult moments ({{MINUTES}})

{{ONE_OR_TWO_USE_CASE_RELEVANT_SCENARIOS_WITH_FIXED_FACTS_AND_PERMITTED_ACTIONS}}

- At each scenario moment, show what the caller says and any confirmed agent action. If capabilities are unknown, compare words and stance without promising an action. Ask "What should the agent say here? What would sound wrong?" Let groups write a short reply and explain their choice. Keep the work to persona-bearing moments; hand a request for a full conversation flow to conversation design.
- Alternatively, offer meaningfully different sample *expressions* (A/B, or A/B/C when useful) and room for participants to **write their own**. State what each choice trades off.
- Vary a deliberate persona decision such as empathy, register, or brevity. Keep facts, safety language, capability, and permitted recommendation constant; flag any policy or workflow disagreement separately for its owner. Park workflow, verification, routing, and tool questions for agent design rather than deciding them as Persona settings.
- Let participants choose or rewrite on their own or in pairs, then discuss the reasons behind disagreement.

### 4. Place positions on shared dimension scales ({{MINUTES}})

{{CHOOSE_A_FEW_UNRESOLVED_DIMENSIONS_AND_DRAW_A_LONG_LABELED_LINE_FOR_EACH}}

- Give each participant a dot or mark per line before discussion. Make the line large enough for multiple positions; include a short, dimension-accurate definition and readable endpoints (plus an optional middle anchor). For a selected dimension, add two or three short response examples on the same facts so participants can hear the tradeoff; use `references/persona-framework.md` for the definition and labels rather than copying another board's scale count.
- Discuss the **spread and reasons**, including any outliers. Dot placement maps positions; it is not automatically a vote or approval.
