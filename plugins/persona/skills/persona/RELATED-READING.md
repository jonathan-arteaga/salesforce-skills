# Persona reading

## Further Reading

These works provide useful context for persona, conversation, language, and Agentforce design. Their inclusion does not imply that this framework derives from them.

### Conversation and Persona Design

- **Deibel, D. & Evanhoe, R., *[Conversations with Things](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/conversations-with-things/)* (Rosenfeld Media)** — UX design for conversational interfaces, including approaches to personification.
- **[Conversation Design Institute](https://conversationdesigninstitute.com/)** — Training and public resources on conversation design and agent personas.
- **Moran, K., [“The Four Dimensions of Tone of Voice”](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tone-of-voice-dimensions/) (Nielsen Norman Group)** — A four-dimension model for analyzing tone in UX writing.
- **Peter, S., Riemer, K., & West, J. D., [“The benefits and dangers of anthropomorphic conversational agents”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12146756/)** — Research on user perceptions of conversational agents.
- **Blevins et al., [“Do language models accommodate their users? A study of linguistic convergence”](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.34/)** — Research on linguistic convergence between language models and users.
- **Joshi et al., [“ELI-Why: Why Is Anything the Way It Is?”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14200)** — Research on adapting explanations to learners' informational needs.

- **[Amazon Alexa Design Guide](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/docs/alexa/alexa-design/get-started.html)** — Conversation-design guidance for spoken experiences.

### Salesforce Documentation

- **[Agentforce Documentation](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/einstein/genai/overview)** — Agentforce platform architecture and design patterns.
- **[Agent Script: Reasoning Instructions](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/ai/agentforce/guide/ascript-ref-instructions.html)** and **[Instruction Overrides](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/ai/agentforce/guide/ascript-patterns-system-overrides.html)** — Instruction hierarchy and subagent calibration surfaces.
- **[How to Speak AI: A Practical Guide to Writing Effective Prompts](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/speak-ai-prompts/)** — Salesforce guidance for prompt authors.
- **[Should You Give Your AI Agent a Human Name?](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/naming-an-ai-agent/)** — Questions to consider when naming an agent.
